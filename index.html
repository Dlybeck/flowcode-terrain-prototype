Current verdict from the real fixtures

Sibling-relative relevance is the leading candidate. On ScribbleScan it makes the locally important steps easier to follow along each execution ridge; the other fixtures remain available to expose cases where that rule becomes misleading.

Keep one project summit as the baseline. Separate summits work only for connected entry basins. Parser-disconnected functions belong at the foothills until the call graph can justify another peak.

Use the textured terrain while testing the rules. The mountain above now rebuilds in Three.js when scoring, summit, or drop controls change.
